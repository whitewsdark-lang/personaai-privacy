Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PersonaAI ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application PersonaAI (the "App").

1. Information We Do Not Collect

PersonaAI is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. Specifically:

  • We do not require account registration or login
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or contact information
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not use advertising networks or third-party analytics SDKs

2. Your Test Results Stay on Your Device

Your quiz answers and personality results are stored locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. We do not upload, back up, or have access to your results history on our servers.

3. How the App Works

When you complete a personality quiz, your answers are sent to our AI processing server solely to generate your personality result. If you choose to add a photo to generate a caricature, that photo is sent to our AI processing servers solely to create your caricature image. This content is processed in real time and is not permanently stored on our servers after your request is complete.

4. Third-Party AI Services

PersonaAI uses the following third-party services to generate content:

  • DeepSeek — used to interpret your quiz answers into a personality result and to generate compatibility readings. See DeepSeek's Privacy Policy.
  • Kie.ai — used only if you choose to upload a photo, to generate a caricature image in a style matching your personality result. See Kie.ai's Privacy Policy.

5. Optional Photo Feature

PersonaAI includes an optional feature that lets you upload or take a photo to generate a caricature. This feature is entirely optional — you can use all personality tests without ever providing a photo. Photos are only used to generate your caricature and are not stored permanently on our servers.

6. Compare with a Friend Feature

PersonaAI lets you optionally invite a friend to take the same quiz on your device, after which we generate a compatibility reading based on both results. This feature does not require any account or personal information from your friend — their quiz answers are processed the same way as your own, and are not stored on our servers after the reading is generated.

7. Device Permissions

PersonaAI may request the following permissions:

  • Camera: To take a photo for the optional caricature feature.
  • Photo library: To choose an existing photo for the optional caricature feature.
  • Internet access: Required to send your quiz answers or photo to our AI services and receive your results.

8. Sharing Feature

PersonaAI lets you optionally share your result as an image through your device's native share function. This image is generated locally on your device and is only shared if and when you choose to do so.

9. Entertainment Purposes

PersonaAI's personality tests, compatibility readings, and caricatures are intended for entertainment and self-reflection purposes. While some tests are based on established psychological frameworks (as noted within the app), results should not be treated as clinical, diagnostic, or professional psychological assessments.

10. Children's Privacy

PersonaAI is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
